# Yesterday Design Specification

## Vision
A private, AI-powered digital family heritage platform that preserves voices, stories, photographs, recipes and memories for generations.

## Design tokens
- Root: #FBF8F3
- Surface: #FFFFFF
- Warm surface: #F4EDE2
- Warm glow: #F9EFE5
- Primary ink: #1F1A16
- Secondary ink: #685F56
- Terra: #9C4A28
- Terra light: #C8683D
- Heritage green: #3A5A40
- Gold: #D4A373
- Border: #EFE8DE

## Product principles
- Audio-first preservation.
- Elder-friendly large touch targets and readable typography.
- Private family archive.
- Offline capture.
- AI suggestions must be verifiable against original source material.
- Never overwrite original recordings.
